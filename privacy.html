Habino Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

The short version: Habino does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no ads.

What Habino stores

Habino stores your habit configurations, completion logs, in-app credits, goals, and game progress locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). If you use Habino on Apple Watch, your habit list and credit totals are synced directly between your iPhone and Watch over Apple's local device-to-device connection. None of this data leaves your devices.

Apple Health data

If you grant permission, Habino reads the following from Apple Health:

  • Activity ring completion status (Move, Exercise, and Stand rings)
  • Standing hour samples from Apple Watch

This data is used solely to award in-app credits when you close your rings or stand during the day. Habino never writes data to Apple Health, and Health data is never transmitted off your device or shared with anyone, including us. If you decline Health access, the app works normally — ring-linked habits simply won't auto-complete.

Notifications

Habino can send you local notifications to remind you about your habits. These are scheduled entirely on your device. No notification tokens or identifiers are sent to any server.

No data collection

Habino does not collect any personal information. There is no account creation, no email address, no login, and no registration of any kind.

No network communication

Habino does not connect to the internet. There are no servers, no APIs, no cloud sync, and no remote storage. The app functions entirely offline.

No analytics or tracking

Habino contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no usage tracking of any kind. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency because there is nothing to track.

No third-party code

Habino is built exclusively with Apple's own frameworks (SwiftUI, SwiftData, HealthKit, UserNotifications, WatchConnectivity). There are no third-party libraries or SDKs embedded in the app.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the Habino app deletes all of your data permanently. There is nothing stored elsewhere to request deletion of.

Children's privacy

Habino does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no mechanisms to collect, store, or transmit personal data of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If Habino's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Since Habino has no way to contact you (we don't have your email), changes will be posted at this URL.
